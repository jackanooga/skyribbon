GET STARTED

Three steps. That's it.

01

Download & unzip

Grab SkyRibbon above and extract the ZIP file.

02

Add to Chrome

Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, then Load unpacked. Select the SkyRibbon folder.

03

Pick your city

Pin SkyRibbon to your toolbar, click it, and search for your city. Your calendar updates automatically.
