Privacy, the short version.

SkyRibbon is a free browser extension. It does not require an account, read your Google Calendar events through an API, or send your calendar contents to SkyRibbon.

Google Calendar access

SkyRibbon runs on Google Calendar pages to add a daily weather ribbon to the interface. It does not need your Google account credentials or Google Calendar API authorization. It adjusts page elements to display forecasts.

Manual city selection

If you search for a city, the search phrase goes to Open-Meteo's geocoding service. The coordinates of your chosen city go to Open-Meteo's forecast service. Your chosen city, temperature units, badge colors, and temporarily cached forecasts are saved locally by your browser extension.

Optional location detection

SkyRibbon requests access to browser geolocation through its extension permissions, but it only reads your location when you click “Use my location” in the popup. It does not continuously track you or check your location in the background.

When you use this feature, the live coordinates obtained from your device are sent directly to BigDataCloud to identify your nearby city and to Open-Meteo to obtain the forecast. BigDataCloud's free client-side service uses coordinate and network signals to help improve its location data; review its privacy policy before enabling the feature. If the city-name lookup fails, SkyRibbon still uses the coordinates for weather and labels the location “Current location.”

Weather details and preferences

Hover and keyboard-focus weather details—including precipitation chance and peak wind—use the same forecast returned by Open-Meteo. No additional request is sent when you hover over a badge. Preferences remain in Chrome's local extension storage, and no SkyRibbon account is required.

Support link

The optional Buy Me a Coffee link opens a third-party website. Any donations are processed by that site, not SkyRibbon.
