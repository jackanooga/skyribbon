PLAIN-LANGUAGE PRIVACY

Privacy, without the clouds.

SkyRibbon is a free browser extension and static website. Neither requires an account. SkyRibbon does not run its own analytics service, advertising service, or backend.

Information the extension uses

SkyRibbon stores your selected city, coordinates, timezone, temperature unit, and recent weather forecasts locally in Chrome extension storage. You can change the city in the extension popup and remove this local information by uninstalling the extension.

Requests to outside services

When you search for a city, your search query is sent to the Open-Meteo Geocoding API. To display the forecast, your chosen location coordinates and temperature unit are sent to the Open-Meteo Weather API. These third-party services may process standard network information such as IP addresses under their own policies.

Google Calendar

The extension adds weather visually to pages at calendar.google.com. It does not request authorization to your Google account, call the Google Calendar API, or transmit your calendar events to SkyRibbon.

Hosting

If deployed on GitHub Pages, GitHub will host the website and may log visitor IP addresses for service security. Refer to GitHub Pages documentation.

Changes
