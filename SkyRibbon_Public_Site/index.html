GOOD TO KNOW

Little questions,
clear skies.

Is it really free?+

Yes. SkyRibbon is a free, noncommercial project. There are no subscriptions, ads, or accounts. The weather is powered by Open-Meteo's free noncommercial API, subject to their limits.

Does it read my Google Calendar events?+

No. SkyRibbon changes how Google Calendar is displayed in your browser to add a forecast strip. It doesn't sign into your Google account or send your calendar events to a server.

Can I change my city later?+

Yes! Open SkyRibbon from the Chrome toolbar and search for another city. You can change the temperature unit there too.

Will it work on my phone?+

Not yet. This extension is designed for desktop Google Calendar in Chrome.

Why do I need Developer mode?+

Publishing through the Chrome Web Store requires a one-time fee. To keep SkyRibbon completely free to launch, you install its unpacked extension manually. Only install software from sources you trust.

How far ahead can I see weather?+

Up to 16 days, depending on the forecast service and which dates are visible in your calendar. Dates outside the forecast window won't show weather badges.
